# 应用图标来源

使用内置 `image_gen` 生成，工具没有开放“image2.5”型号选择。原始图集 `atlas-original.png`，项目使用八张裁剪后的 PNG；机身使用同源 80×80 RGB565A8 图像。

提示词：
Create one production-ready UI icon sprite atlas for a tiny robot display. A precisely aligned grid of 4 columns and 2 rows, 8 icons total, landscape 2:1 canvas, all cells identical square size. Each cell has solid pure black background #000000 and the icon occupies the centered 78% square region with generous equal black gutters. All eight icons must have the SAME squircle silhouette and corner radius, inspired by the polished colorful minimal app icons of Xiaomi HyperOS, but ORIGINAL artwork with NO Xiaomi logos. Soft rich gradient tile colors, clean bold central pictograms with gentle depth and tiny highlight, extremely clear at 64x64, no text or numbers or labels, no shadows extending outside the tile. Precise order left to right: TOP ROW (1) friendly white robot eyes on cobalt blue tile, (2) Chinese wooden fish percussion instrument with small mallet on warm amber tile, (3) white six-sided die with dark pips on coral orange tile, (4) four small sliding puzzle squares on fresh green tile. BOTTOM ROW (5) clean camera lens on violet tile, (6) analog clock face on cyan blue tile, (7) shiny white marble and green target ring on turquoise tile, (8) white settings gear on cool slate gray tile. Flat front-facing view, symmetric composition within each tile, consistent restrained dimensional shading. No phone frame, no interface mockup, no typography, no outlines around grid, no checkerboard, no extra icons. The complete atlas will be cropped into individual app icons.
